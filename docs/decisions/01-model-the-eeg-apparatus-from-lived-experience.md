# Model the EEG apparatus from lived experience

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

Using the user's undergraduate apparatus as the primary source, what components, signals, procedure stages, adaptive decisions, common failures, and success or abort conditions must the prototype represent to feel scientifically recognizable? Resolve whether the apparatus was scalp EEG, implanted electrophysiology, or another modality, and explicitly separate authentic details from demo fiction.

## Answer

Model a configurable whole-cap **human scalp-EEG apparatus**, seeded by the user's TDT acquisition, optical-trigger, and custom-response topology but independent of the original auditory-localization paradigm. Make qualitative trace and frequency inspection central; require targeted remediation and retesting; and clearly label synthetic signals, thresholds, fault outcomes, and verifier rules as demo fiction.
