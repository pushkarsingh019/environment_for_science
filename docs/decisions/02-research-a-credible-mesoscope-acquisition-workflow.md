Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

From primary scientific, manufacturer, and software documentation, what minimum two-photon mesoscope acquisition-readiness workflow can be simulated credibly and visually without presenting the prototype as operational laser-control guidance? Identify the apparatus components, observables, bounded actions, interlocks, likely faults, success conditions, and source citations that later design work may rely on.

## Answer

Use a sealed **Four-region handoff** mock acquisition: validate independent interlocks and immutable ROI, trigger, and logging contracts, then accept or quarantine a watermarked synthetic package without exposing optical, detector, motion, alignment, or calibration controls.
